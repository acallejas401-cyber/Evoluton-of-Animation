THE BIG IDEA — 01

Animation is the illusion of movement.

Animation creates the feeling of movement by showing a sequence of still images in quick succession.

Although animation is strongly associated with computers today, its foundations are much older. Early humans represented movement in cave paintings, and later inventors used optical devices to explore how separate images could appear to move.

Over time, artists combined drawing, timing, sound, color, storytelling, and new production tools. Each technical change opened new creative possibilities while keeping the same central idea: bringing images to life.

〰BEFORE THE FRAME

The Origins of Animation

Long before cinema, people used images to represent movement. Cave paintings are an early example of this interest. Later, optical experiments helped explain how separate images could create an illusion of motion.

01 — 08
02 / MARIANA◎
THE MAGIC OF PERSISTENCE

Optical Devices & Early Films

The thaumatrope, zoetrope, praxinoscope, and flipbook explored the relationship between still images and movement. In 1908, Émile Cohl created Fantasmagorie; in 1914, Winsor McCay presented Gertie the Dinosaur.

02 — 08
03 / MOZ✦
SOUND · COLOR · STORY

The Golden Age

From the late 1920s through the 1960s, animation became more popular. Steamboat Willie (1928) helped popularize synchronized sound, while color made animated productions more expressive and attractive to audiences.

03 — 08
04 / BRITTANY▧
STUDIOS BUILD WORLDS

Studios & Animated Films

Disney, Fleischer Studios, and Warner Bros. helped shape the industry with memorable characters and productions. Disney's Snow White and the Seven Dwarfs (1937) became an important feature-length animated film.

04 — 08
05 / ANGEL◇
FROM PIXELS TO WORLDS

The Digital Revolution

At the end of the 20th century, computers changed how artists produced and edited animation. CGI, 2D digital animation, and 3D workflows became important tools. Programs such as Toon Boom Harmony, Adobe Animate, Blender, and Autodesk Maya expanded the production toolkit.

05 — 08
06 / NATALIA⌁
REALITY, REIMAGINED

Contemporary Animation

Streaming has made animated content available to global audiences in many formats. Motion capture, artificial intelligence, virtual reality, augmented reality, and real-time rendering continue to change how animation is created and experienced.

06 — 08
07 / SOFIA✳
ARTISTS MOVE THE MEDIUM

Important Contributors

Émile Cohl and Winsor McCay helped establish early animation. Walt Disney helped animation grow into a major entertainment industry. Hayao Miyazaki, John Lasseter, and Glen Keane also influenced the medium through artistic and technical work.

07 — 08
08 / MARCELA◉
STORIES WITHOUT BORDERS

Impact & Conclusion

Animation is used in films, television, streaming, video games, and advertising. It supports creative careers, helps stories cross cultural boundaries, and influences popular culture. Its history shows how art and technology continually shape entertainment.
